# Phase 2: DSR Operationalization Framework
**Project**: IDG Competency Framework for Human-Centered Digital Transformation
---
## Step 1: Analysis of Phase 1 Context
**Artifact Goal**: Construct a **Framework of IDG-based competencies** required by technical leaders to successfully drive human-centered digital transformation.
**Investigation Aspects** (building blocks to extract from literature):
1. What competencies/skills define "inner development" for leaders in digital contexts?
1. What existing frameworks model leadership capabilities for digital transformation?
1. What design principles guide competency framework construction (DSR perspective)?
1. What theories explain the role of the human factor in organizational digital change?
---
## Step 2: Inclusion / Exclusion Criteria
**Mandatory Content (Inclusion)**:
- Must provide prescriptive content: design principles, framework components, or capability taxonomies
- Must address leadership, inner skills, or human-factor competencies in a digital/organizational transformation context
- Must be applicable to technical leadership roles (not purely educational/pedagogical)
- Empirical validation OR theoretical grounding for the proposed constructs
**Out-of-Scope (Exclusion)**:
- Purely technical papers (architecture, software engineering) without leadership/human competency component
- Descriptive problem analyses without proposed solutions or frameworks
- SDG/sustainability papers focusing on technological tools rather than human capacity
- General HRM papers with no link to digital transformation
