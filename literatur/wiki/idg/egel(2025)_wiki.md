# GLfS and IDGs: An Integrated Framework and Roadmap

**Authors:** Eleftheria Egel, Louis W. (Jody) Fry, Mauricio Campos Suarez
**Venue:** Book chapter (Chapter 11), DOI: 10.1515/9783111453729-012
**Method:** First principles thinking (conceptual/theoretical)

---

## Research Objective

Integrate the Inner Development Goals (IDGs) within a model of Global Leadership for Sustainability (GLfS) to produce a roadmap for leader and leadership development that addresses the root causes of sustainability failure.

## Core Argument: Sustainability 1.0 → 2.0

The paper uses first principles thinking in three stages:

| Stage | Question | Modus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **A** | Why do leaders struggle to implement TBL despite passion? | Sustainability 1.0 (current system) |
| **B** | How can we reshape building blocks for a coherent sustainability perspective? | Sustainability 2.0 (target system) |
| **C** | How can we act on this novel perspective? | Transition via GLfS + IDGs |

### Sustainability 1.0 (current)
Two building blocks: (1) physical world is prevailing reality → materialism/consumerism; (2) economy is separate from society → utility maximization, competition. Leadership operates at Levels V and IV (trait-based, charismatic/transformational). Result: superficial sustainability despite TBL rhetoric.

### Sustainability 2.0 (target)
Reshaped building blocks: (1) world is life-enhancing ecology → holism, interconnectedness; (2) economy is embedded in society → collaboration, value rationality. Leadership operates at Levels III, II, and I (conscious, spiritual, non-dual).

## Being-Centered Leadership (BCL) — Levels of Being

Based on Fry & Kriger (2009), a developmental hierarchy of leadership consciousness:

| Level | Leadership type | Key attributes | State of consciousness | Organizational goal |
|-------|----------------|---------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **V** | Trait, situational, transactional | Having and doing | Shaped by five senses | Acquire resources efficiently |
| **IV** | Charismatic, narcissistic, transformational | Compelling vision, strong cultural values | Shaped by social/cultural context | Foster inclusive environments, navigate social dynamics |
| **III** | Conscious, responsible, ethical, authentic, relational | Self and other-awareness | Rooted in subjective felt experience of the present | Awaken leaders' capacity to lead wisely and lovingly |
| **II** | Spiritual, servant, global leadership for sustainability | Self-transcendence and interconnectedness | Connection to deeper, holistic understanding of systemic reality | Prioritize ethical considerations, long-term sustainability, compassion |
| **I** | Non-dual | Oneness | Rooted in pure emptiness and pure completeness | (aspirational) |

Key insight: Sustainability 1.0 leaders function at Levels V and IV. Sustainability 2.0 requires Levels III and II. **The transition requires inner development — which is exactly what the IDG framework provides.**

## The GLfS Framework

GLfS (Fry & Egel, 2021) is grounded in being-centered leadership theory and spiritual leadership theory. It requires:
- **Global Mindset for Sustainability (GMS)** — a presencing practice (Level III+ of being)
- **Hope/Faith** in a vision for sustainability
- **Altruistic Love** — organizational culture based on love and service
- **Purpose** — calling/meaning, making a difference
- **Community** — membership/belonging for sustainability
- Outcomes: spiritual well-being → leadership for sustainability → organizational commitment, productivity, life satisfaction → **Triple Bottom Line (TBL)**

## IDG Integration into GLfS (Figure 11.5)

The paper maps all 5 IDG dimensions onto the GLfS model:

| IDG Dimension | Skills | GLfS Integration Point |
|---------------|--------|----------------------|
| **Being** (1) | Self-awareness, presence, inner compass, integrity, openness/learning mindset | Foundation: GMS presencing practice; supports developing and sustaining GMS |
| **Thinking** (2) | Critical thinking, long-term orientation, perspective skills, sensemaking, complexity awareness | Vision for sustainability; cognitive skills for systems thinking |
| **Relating** (3) | Appreciation, connectedness, humility, empathy, compassion | Altruistic love; caring for others and the world |
| **Collaborating** (4) | Trust, inclusive mindset, intercultural competence, communication, co-creation, mobilization | Community; social skills for collective action |
| **Acting** (5) | Creativity, courage, optimism, perseverance | Enabling change; driving meaningful action |

**Being is foundational** — it is the prerequisite for all other dimensions. GMS (developed through inner life practice at Level III+) is what enables leadership for sustainability.

## The 23 IDG Skills (listed)

- **Being:** inner compass, integrity/authenticity, openness/learning mindset, self-awareness/presence
- **Thinking:** critical thinking, complexity awareness, perspective skills, sensemaking, long-term orientation/visioning
- **Relating:** appreciation, connectedness, humility, empathy/compassion
- **Collaborating:** communication skills, co-creation skills, inclusive mindset/intercultural competence, trust, mobilization skills
- **Acting:** courage, creativity, optimism, perseverance
