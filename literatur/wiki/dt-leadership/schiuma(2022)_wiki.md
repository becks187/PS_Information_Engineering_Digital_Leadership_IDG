# Schiuma, Schettini, Santarsiero & Carlucci (2022) — Transformative Leadership Compass

**Journal:** International Journal of Entrepreneurial Behavior & Research, Vol. 28 No. 5, pp. 1273–1291  
**DOI:** 10.1108/IJEBR-01-2021-0087  
**Citations:** 208  
**Type:** Conceptual paper (literature review synthesis — NOT empirical, NOT DSR)

---

## Summary

Schiuma et al. synthesize three leadership streams — wise leadership (Nonaka & Takeuchi), transformative leadership, and digital leadership — to propose the **Transformative Leadership Compass**: a descriptive framework of six competencies distinguishing the "Digital Transformative Leader." The central construct is **digital transformation entrepreneurship (DTE)**: the organizational attitude of continuously creating and developing business by having digital transformation at its core. The paper is the most-cited leadership competency framework in the DT corpus (208 citations) but is entirely conceptual — no empirical validation, no DSR artifact process.

---

## The Six Competencies (Transformative Leadership Compass)

Each competency is anchored by a defining question and a leadership role archetype:

| # | Competency | Role | Core Question |
|---|-----------|------|---------------|
| 1 | **Grasping the Essence of the Digital Transformation** | Pragmatic | *What is the digital transformation for?* |
| 2 | **Envisioning Digital Wealth Creation Goodness** | Righteous | *What are the wealth creation goals?* |
| 3 | **Shaping a Knowledge-Creating Context** | Facilitator | *How to generate growth spaces of digital knowledge?* |
| 4 | **Communicating the Essence of Digital Transformation** | Communicator | *How to inspire people to create new DT knowledge?* |
| 5 | **Engaging People to Act with Digital Transformation** | Catalyst | *How to energize and lead people to act?* |
| 6 | **Making Digital Transformation Everyone's Job** | Mentor | *How to foster digital transformative competencies in others?* |

### Competency details

**1. Grasping the Essence** — connecting digital knowledge with business challenges; pragmatic problem-solving; critical thinking; seeing both trees and forest simultaneously.

**2. Envisioning Goodness** — ethical, values-driven decision-making; thinking beyond profit toward societal good; nurturing optimism, idealism, and righteousness; spiritual well-being.

**3. Shaping Knowledge Context** — creating BA (knowledge spaces, Nonaka & Takeuchi); building open, learning-oriented environments; facilitating creative collisions; well-being as foundation for knowledge work.

**4. Communicating the Essence** — storytelling, metaphors, rhetoric; building personal bonds and organizational identity; mastering logos, pathos, ethos; inspiring through narrative.

**5. Engaging People** — exercising "political power" to mobilize diverse people; dialectical thinking (embracing contradictions/paradoxes); empathy; charismatic energy and commitment.

**6. Making Everyone's Job** — distributing practical wisdom; empowering and mentoring others; nurturing critical thinking throughout the organization; enabling employee-level DT capability.

## Three Leadership Streams Synthesized

| Stream | Core concept | Key source |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Wise leadership | *Phronesis* — practical wisdom enabling ethically sound judgment | Nonaka & Takeuchi (2011, 2019) |
| Transformative leadership | Ethically-based model integrating values and stakeholder outcomes | Caldwell et al. (2010, 2012) |
| Digital leadership | Digital vision, knowledge, fail fast, empowerment, diverse team management | El Sawy et al. (2020); Cortellazzo et al. (2019) |

---

## Key Claims

- A "digital transformative leader" is not just a digital leader — they must be simultaneously wise (phronetic), transformative (ethical, stakeholder-oriented), and digitally capable.
- DT is fundamentally about **talent and soft skills**, not technology (*"Digital transformation is about talent, not technology"* — Frankiewicz & Chamorro-Premuzic, 2020).
- Leadership must spread DT as a **shared organizational culture**, not a top-down mandate.
- The framework is descriptive — intended as a theoretical foundation for future empirical research and assessment instruments.

---

## Critical Limitations (stated by authors)

- **Conceptual only** — grounded-based theory, deductive analysis; no inductive empirical testing.
- Authors plan semi-structured interviews to validate and refine (future work).
- No operationalized measurement items.
- Does not target specific technical leadership roles.

## Hidden IDG Alignment (your key analytical move)

| Shaping Knowledge Context | **Collaborating** — co-creation, community building |
| Communicating the Essence | **Relating** — communication, empathy, connection |
| Engaging People | **Relating + Collaborating** — empathy, mobilization |
| Making Everyone's Job | **Acting** — embodying change, enabling others |
