# Philip, Gilli & Knappstein (2023) — Cross-Sectoral Delphi Study

**Journal:** Leadership & Organization Development Journal, Vol. 44 No. 3, 2023, pp. 392–406  
**DOI:** 10.1108/LODJ-02-2022-0063  
**Citations:** 42  
**Type:** Empirical — Lockean-led Delphi study (18 international senior managers, 3 rounds)

---

## Summary

Philip et al. recruited 18 senior managers from across industries and countries (Europe, North America, Asia, Australia) and ran a 3-round Delphi survey to empirically identify and rank DT leadership competencies. Starting from 34 items, consensus converged on **39 items** organized into **7 clusters**. The central finding: **behavioral competencies collectively outweigh strategic competencies** in driving DT. This is among the most methodologically rigorous empirical validations of DT leadership competency lists in the corpus.

---

## The 7 Clusters (ranked by importance — lower weighted sum = more important)

| Rank | Cluster | Type | Weighted sum | Key items |
|------|---------|------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Leadership** | Behavioral | 13 | Instilling Trust, Creating Purpose, Leading in Uncertain Times, Enabling People, Listening, Influencing |
| 2 | **Strategy** | Strategic | 31 | Visionary Thinking, Knowledge about Strategy |
| 3 | **Collaboration** | Behavioral | 38 | Knowledge Sharing, Customer Centricity, Co-Creation, Team Orientation, Relationship-Building |
| 4 | **Personality Traits** | Behavioral | 48 | Honesty, Passion, Openness, Resilience, **Empathy**, Curiosity, Proactiveness, Courage, Flexibility, **Self-Awareness**, Social Competence |
| 5 | **Change Management** | Strategic | 50 | Agility, Accept Change |
| 6 | **Data Management** | Strategic | 59 | Understanding Value of Data, Data-Driven Decision-Making |
| 7 | **Skills** | Behavioral | 66 | Holistic Thinking, Communication, Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, **Systems Thinking**, Presentation |

**Top 3 individual items by rank:** Visionary Thinking (15), Agility (15), Understanding Value of Data (15)

Ratio: 4 behavioral clusters vs 3 strategic clusters. Leadership cluster ranked #1.

---

## Two Core Propositions

**Proposition 1:** Organizational leaders driving DT should possess a combination of behavioral *and* strategic competencies.

**Proposition 2:** Behavioral competencies are collectively more important than strategic ones. Contextual variations in national culture and industry sector dictate *which* behavioral competencies are prioritized.

---

## Key Claims

- **People are the key to DT, not technology** (Kane, 2019 — confirmed empirically here).
- Leadership (trust, purpose, enabling people) is the most important competency cluster overall.
- Self-awareness, empathy, and resilience are peer-validated essential inner traits — not soft add-ons.
- Context matters significantly: Western consulting firms value individual skills; African/Asian communal cultures prioritize collaboration.
- Systems thinking is recognized by practitioners as critical but "currently still a very academic understanding" — gap between theory and practice.

## Standout Expert Quotes (direct from practitioners)

> "Leading in VUCA times also requires a high level of self-awareness and self-care as you are constantly under stress and need to make sure you nurture yourself otherwise you risk burning out. This is a topic that comes up amongst informal meetings of leaders, it's not something published or advertised... but essential to be successful."

> "I would add empathy to the list, often lacking, been driven out of people through outdated focus on results, technology, process etc."

> "Emotional Intelligence — Understand potential fears or barriers of people that avoid Digital Transformation."

> "Leaders (should) try to add balance through meditation and yoga as it trains mindfulness."

These comments point directly to **IDG Being dimension** content — but practitioners have no structured framework to express it. This is exactly the gap your artifact fills.

## Delphi Methodology (directly applicable to your expert validation phase)

| Round | Purpose | Result |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Round 1 | Collect items; open-ended additions | 13/18 responded (72%); 82 items generated |
| Round 2 | Rate 47 items on 5-point Likert scale | 12/18 responded (67%); 39 items shortlisted |
| Round 3 | Rank within and between clusters | 11/18 responded (61%); final ranking |

Consensus criterion: item rated "very important" or "absolute essential" by ≥70% of participants.  
Expert panel: 18 managers across ICT consulting, strategy consulting, consumer goods, telecom, automotive, financial services, higher education.

| Philip cluster | IDG dimension |

| Skills (systems thinking, holistic thinking, critical thinking) | **Thinking** |
| Leadership (trust, listening, enabling people) | **Relating** |
| Collaboration (co-creation, team orientation, knowledge sharing) | **Collaborating** |

The Delphi experts empirically confirm all five IDG dimensions — but without the IDG framework to name them. This paper provides empirical grounding for your IDG-based artifact.
