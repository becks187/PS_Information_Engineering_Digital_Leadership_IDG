# Blanka, Krumay & Rueckel (2022) — DigiTransComp

**Journal:** Technological Forecasting & Social Change 178 (2022) 121575  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.techfore.2022.121575  
**Citations:** 202

---

## Summary

Blanka et al. apply [[concept-design-science-research|Design Science Research]] to develop the **DigiTransComp framework** — a competency model mapping *intrapreneurial* and *digital* employee competencies onto five levels of IT-enabled business transformation (adapted from Venkatraman 1994). The core argument is that digitalization is a *human* transformation challenge, not merely a technological one: employees must possess intrapreneurial competencies to proactively shape digital transformation rather than just being affected by it. The framework was developed in two DSR iterations using expert interviews (10 interviewees from academia and practice).

---

## Key Claims

				- **Digital competency alone is insufficient.** Basic digital literacy (information, media, technology skills) is necessary at all levels, but employees also need *intrapreneurial* competencies to exploit digitalization opportunities
- **Intrapreneurial competency is dynamic.** It develops in iterative cycles of intent → action → implementation, not linearly. Individual learning feeds organizational capability.
- **Competency requirements scale with transformation level.** Lower levels (localized exploitation, internal integration) require management insight and proactiveness; revolutionary levels (business network/scope redesign) additionally require market foresight and interpersonal mobilization.
- **Human capital is the key enabler.** Organizations advance from one level of DT to the next primarily through employee competency development — not through technology alone.

## The DigiTransComp Framework

Two axes:
- Y-axis: **Level of Digital Transformation** (evolutionary → revolutionary)
- X-axis: **Benefit of Digital Transformation** (low → high)

Five transformation levels (Venkatraman 1994):
1. Localized Exploitation
2. Internal Integration
3. Business Process Redesign
4. Business Network Redesign
5. Business Scope Redefinition

**Six intrapreneurial competency clusters** (mapped onto transformation levels):

| Cluster | Key competencies |
|---------|-----------------|
| Management Insight | Strategic planning, vision, coping with ambiguity |
| Proactiveness | Initiative, self-awareness, sustainable thinking |
| Idea Generation | Creativity, applying innovation, spotting opportunities |
| Opportunity Evaluation | Environment scanning, valuing ideas, strategy formulation |
| Interpersonal Mobilization | Interaction, consensus-building, collaboration |
| Market Foresight | Market development, identifying niches, product innovation |

Plus transversal: **Information, Media & Technology Skills** (vertical, required at all levels)

## DSR Method (Directly Applicable to Your Paper)

Follows Peffers et al. (2007) DSRM process:

| DSR Activity | How Blanka applied it |
|---|---|
| 1. Problem identification | Literature gap: no framework linking DT levels with employee competency |
| 2. Solution objectives | Framework integrating digital + intrapreneurial competency |
| 3. Design & development | Literature review → initial framework (Fig. 3) |
| 4. Demonstration | Expert interviews (Iteration 1: 6 experts in SM, IS, HR) |
| 5. Evaluation | Second iteration (4 experts in INM, OD) → redesigned framework (Fig. 4) |
| 6. Communication | Published article |

**Evaluation criteria used:** Validity, utility, quality, and efficacy (Gregor & Hevner 2013). Experts assessed: ease of understanding, accuracy of DT overview, practical applicability.

**Expert selection:** 10 interviews across academia and practice; two iterations; semi-structured format with framework exposure in second part.

## Gap / Your Contribution

Blanka's framework covers employees broadly — it does not:
- Target **technical leadership roles** (CTO, IT manager, DT lead)
- Use **IDG taxonomy** (Being, Thinking, Relating, Collaborating, Acting) as organizing principle
- Operationalize **inner capacities** (presence, systems thinking, self-awareness) beyond generic intrapreneurial clusters
